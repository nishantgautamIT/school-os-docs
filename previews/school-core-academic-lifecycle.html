F05 · 3 November 2026

Open a year.
Preserve every enrollment transition.

Protected operator workflow for opening and closing academic years, moving active students between sections, and recording withdrawal and re-entry.

Year lifecycle

activate_year changes a draft year to active and all planned enrollments to active in one transaction. close_year changes an active year to closed and its active enrollments to completed. Cancelled and withdrawn records stay unchanged. Neither action promotes students into another year, changes school activation, grants login access, or runs on a date schedule. Multiple academic years may be active; all operations name an explicit year.

Year commands require academicYearId, expectedVersion and expectedEnrolments (the exact number of planned or active rows being transitioned). The current roster is revalidated under the school lock. Matching counts do not certify an unchanged roster; this applies the current valid roster. Maximum 10,000 transitioned rows per command; larger years reject atomically until a separately designed bulk workflow exists. Empty years are supported with an explicit count of zero.

Enrollment lifecycle

  • transfer_enrolment: active to active, with explicit destination section and rollNumber, within the same active year.
  • withdraw_enrolment: active to withdrawn; keeps the annual ID, section and roll in history, and releases the active seat and roll reservation.
  • restore_enrolment: withdrawn to active with an explicit section and rollNumber. Requires a registered student and available capacity/roll.

Each command requires enrolmentId and expectedVersion. Transfer/restore require sectionId and rollNumber (string or null). Cancelled draft plans remain cancelled after activation; late admissions and conversion of cancelled plans into live enrollments are later work. Closed years cannot reopen or accept enrollment edits. Completed means the enrollment year was closed, not that the student passed an exam or was promoted.

Persistence and ownership

Forward migration 0008 extends people.enrolments states and the enrollment history action constraint. Planned and active rows share a unique section/roll reservation; withdrawn, cancelled and completed rows release it. Existing same-school/year foreign keys remain. Two private forced-RLS tables, academics.lifecycle_commands and academics.year_events, store redacted retry receipts and versioned year history. Enrollment changes continue to append to people.enrolment_events. Runtime and dispatcher receive no grants; no HTTP routes or identity links are added.

Concurrency and atomicity

All operations require school_migrator, a valid tenant context and provisioning/active school state. A request lock ensures exact retries return the original receipt; changed reuse conflicts. The shared school row lock serializes planning, imports, capacity edits, activation, closing and enrollment transitions. Expected versions fence stale changes. Activation rejects inactive students, invalid states and excess capacity; transfers/restoration validate registered students and same-year sections. Withdrawal and closing can clean up records for inactive students.

Year and enrollment versions, histories, audit and receipt commit together. Any failure rolls back all writes. Year results contain state/version and transition count; enrollment results contain IDs, placement metadata and versions. No names/admission codes are echoed. SQL administrators remain privileged. Existing teaching assignments are retained; ending them is an explicit separate operation.

Operator contract

npm run academics:lifecycle -- /private/command.json. All commands contain action, tenantId, requestId, operatorRef and reason. Files must be private regular files with no group/other access, at most 16 KiB. Unknown fields, malformed identifiers, invalid versions and unsupported transitions reject. Replays remain available after later transitions or school suspension.
