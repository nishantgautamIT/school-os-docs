WhatsApp integration decision · checked 22 October 2026

Free allowance and paid WhatsApp messaging

Use one official API integration. Apply eligible free allowance first, then paid delivery within the school's approved budget.

Free is a message-pricing outcome, not a second bulk API. A 100-recipient template campaign can be chargeable while eligible service replies use a remaining allowance. Classify each recipient/message before estimating a campaign.

The current allowance

Meta's dated pricing FAQ says that, from 1 October 2026, each business phone number receives 1,000 free service messages monthly; subsequent service messages are billed. Direct platform access has no additional Meta access fee. Partner charges may still apply. Meta FAQ — pricing answer.

The allowance is for eligible service replies. It is not 1,000 parents, campaigns, conversations or arbitrary templates. Outbound school templates need their applicable tariff. Gupshup's October update also confirms that utility templates inside the customer-service window are chargeable. Gupshup October pricing update.

Use the dated October 2026 pricing answer; some public summary pages retain older free-window or conversation-based wording. Exact India rates and tier treatment must come from the sender's effective rate card/account, not an old blog or this prototype.

Personal-number WhatsApp Web extensions

Yes: third-party browser extensions advertise small free sending allowances through a logged-in WhatsApp Web account. These are the extension vendor's quotas, separate from Meta's official API service allowance. The following are observed vendor claims, not code/security audits or endorsements.

ExtensionPublished free allowance checked 22 October 2026Evidence
WAsurgeUp to 30 messages per day.Vendor FAQ and Chrome Web Store listing. Describes WhatsApp Web sending and batching. Vendor safety/anti-block claims have not been verified.
WAWebSender5 messages per day on its currently opened pricing page.Vendor pricing. Search-index snippets showed a different promotional bonus; use live account terms rather than relying on cached promotions.

Batching does not multiply an allowance. With a 30-per-day plan, three batches of 10 consume the same 30-message daily quota. Another batch does not automatically receive another free allowance. This cannot promise a same-day school-wide send at that free tier.

WhatsApp's responsible-use guidance warns that unauthorised automated/bulk messaging can lead to account bans. A vendor's small daily cap or “anti-ban” claim does not establish permission or account safety. WhatsApp responsible-use guidance; current messaging guidelines.

Product decision: keep the automated multi-school delivery path on an official integration. A browser extension is a separately operated client tool, not a verified drop-in API fallback. Before considering any school-data use, review its actual permissions/data handling and vendor terms; no extension has been installed, connected or used in this research.

How the backend will choose

School sender + permitted audience→Opt-in / opt-out + relationship→Template or eligible service reply→Price + allowance + provider fee→Reserve budget / hold→Queue + callback reconciliation
  1. For single or bulk sends, freeze approved content and eligible recipient snapshot. Each recipient gets a private delivery record; teachers can target only authorised audiences.
  2. Use the school's configured official sender. A parent message opens/resets the customer-service window; sending a notification does not. Meta requires approved templates outside that window. Record messaging permission and honour opt-out. WhatsApp messaging policy.
  3. Classify cost using the effective tariff and the sender's reconciled usage. Reserve remaining allowance atomically across workers. Eligibility is independent of how many recipients are in the batch.
  4. If a message is estimated to have zero Meta fee, still account for provider fees. Paid sending proceeds only when enabled and within school budget; otherwise mark it awaiting_budget. Unknown prices or unresolved category require review.
  5. Submit in bounded worker batches, respecting account/provider throughput and quality restrictions. Retries are idempotent where supported; ambiguous acceptance is reconciled before resending.
  6. Verify and durably receive callbacks; preserve sent/delivered/read/failed evidence, category, billable reason, charge currency and amounts when available. Reconcile invoices where callbacks omit amounts. A read is not consent, homework completion or payment.

Actual charges and free-tier accounting come from the configured provider/Meta billing evidence. Local estimates can lag other clients using the same number. Preserve usage across provider migrations and month boundaries; do not assume every month starts at a hardcoded server timezone.

Database additions

Reuse comms.notices, approved content/audience tables, per-parent deliveries, attempts, events and durable quotas. Add these WhatsApp-specific records:

  • whatsapp_senders — school sender mapping and paid-sending budget controls.
  • whatsapp_templates — template/language versions, approved category and live status.
  • channel_permission_events — opt-in/out evidence tied to school, purpose and contact.
  • whatsapp_threads — inbound window summary without treating a phone match as identity proof.
  • whatsapp_inbound_messages — deduplicated replies and handling evidence.
  • whatsapp_pricing_versions — effective tariffs, allowance configuration and provider fees.
  • whatsapp_sender_usage_months — monthly sender usage/reservations and billing reconciliation.
  • whatsapp_delivery_payloads — exact approved send mode, template parameters and pricing reason.

Guardian recipients can exist without a portal membership. Receiving a message never grants access to another child, a confidential report or a payment/consent action.

Before a live school campaign

Confirm sender ownership/onboarding, approved template languages/categories, parent messaging permissions, exact active tariff and allowance rules, provider fees and budget. Exercise duplicate/out-of-order callbacks, opt-out during a queued campaign, stale templates, concurrent allowance claims, paid-budget exhaustion and failed-send reconciliation. No live WhatsApp account has been connected or charged by this design exercise.
